# Plans and architecture decisions

Read [`../plans/README.md`](../plans/README.md) before creating or updating an
implementation plan. Read [`../adrs/README.md`](../adrs/README.md) before
creating or updating an ADR. Use their templates and maintain their indexes.

Plans are living delivery documents while active. ADRs capture durable decisions
and their rationale. Do not hide unresolved architectural choices inside a plan:
create proposed ADRs and link them. Do not create ADRs for routine local details.

When implementation diverges materially from an active plan, update the plan
before continuing. Close plans with their actual result and verification rather
than merely changing the status.
